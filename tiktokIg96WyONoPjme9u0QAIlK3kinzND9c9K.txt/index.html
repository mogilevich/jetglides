What we do

JetGlides produces short cinematic clips of flights flown inside Microsoft Flight Simulator — real-world routes, real aircraft, recorded in-sim — and publishes them to our own social channels (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok). Every frame is simulated footage; nothing depicts real passengers, real flights, or real people.

  • Automated recording of scenic simulated flights
  • Cinematic editing with royalty-free music
  • Automated publishing to our own social accounts

Integrations

We use official platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API) solely to upload our own generated videos to our own accounts. We do not act on behalf of, or collect data from, any other users.
