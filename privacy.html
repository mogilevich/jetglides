Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JetGlides project (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with our website and our use of social-media platform APIs.

1. We do not collect personal data from visitors

Our website is a set of static informational pages. It does not use accounts, tracking cookies, analytics, or forms, and it does not collect personal information from people who view it.

2. Platform API access

JetGlides uses official platform APIs — the YouTube Data API, the Instagram Graph API, and the TikTok Content Posting API — for a single purpose: to upload videos that we generate to our own social-media accounts. We authenticate only with our own accounts via each platform’s OAuth flow.

3. Access tokens

OAuth access tokens issued for our own accounts are stored privately on our own server and are used only to publish our own content. Tokens are never shared with third parties and are not exposed publicly. We do not request access to, or store, data belonging to any other user.

4. Data we do not process

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data about viewers, followers, or any other individuals. The videos we publish contain only computer-generated flight-simulator footage.

5. Data retention & deletion

Because we store only our own OAuth tokens and generated video files, there is no third-party personal data to retain or delete. Our own tokens can be revoked at any time from the respective platform’s settings.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this Policy: jetglides24@gmail.com
