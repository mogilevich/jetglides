Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the JetGlides project (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with our website and our use of social-media platform APIs.

1. Website visitors

Our website is a set of static informational pages. It does not use accounts, tracking cookies, analytics, or forms, and it does not collect personal information from people who view it.

2. Platform API access

JetGlides uses official platform APIs — YouTube (Data API and Analytics API), Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login), Facebook (Pages API) and TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API). We sign in only with our own accounts, via each platform’s OAuth flow, and use these APIs to:

  • publish the videos we generate to our own accounts;
  • read statistics of our own videos and accounts (views, likes, watch time, follower counts) — these are aggregate numbers and do not identify individual viewers;
  • read public comments left on our own videos, so that we can reply to them and fly the routes viewers ask for.

3. Comments on our videos

When someone comments on one of our videos, we store a copy of that public comment on our own server: the commenter’s public username or display name and platform user ID, the comment text, its time and like count, and which of our videos it was posted under. TikTok comments are not available to us through an API; we copy them by hand when we want to respond.

We use this information only to reply to the comment and to recognise route requests (for example, “fly Athens to Corfu”). To draft a reply or understand a request, the comment text and the commenter’s public username may be sent to an AI language-model service (Google Gemini). We review AI-drafted replies before posting them; the only automatic reply is a short template message telling a viewer that the route they requested is ready. We do not use comment data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

If we fly a route someone asked for, the caption of that video may credit the requester by their public username on the same platform (“Requested by @name”). Ask us and we will remove the credit.

4. Access tokens

OAuth access tokens issued for our own accounts are stored privately on our own server. They are used only for the purposes listed above, are never shared with third parties, and are not exposed publicly.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Apart from the AI drafting described in section 3, comment data stays on our own server.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep stored comments only while we need them to manage replies and route requests. Deleting your comment on the platform does not remove our stored copy automatically, so to have your data deleted, email jetglides24@gmail.com with your username and the platform. We will delete your comments and any credit in our captions within 30 days and confirm by email. Our own tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform’s settings.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this Policy: jetglides24@gmail.com
